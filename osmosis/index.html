FAQs

The Answers You Need Before You Join

Who is Osmosis for? ▼

Osmosis is for anyone who wants to keep learning the markets live — without committing to an intensive curriculum right now. Whether you're newer and want to absorb by being around it, or you've got some experience and just want the calls, the community, and real access to me, this is your room. If you're in a season where structure isn't the priority but staying close to the market is, Osmosis fits.

How is this different from a signals group? ▼

Signals groups hand you a ticker and a "good luck." Osmosis is a learning room — you get the reasoning behind every trade, live Q&A where I actually answer you, and full transparency on my real trades (wins and losses). The whole point is to make you understand the market yourself, not to keep you dependent on alerts.

Do I need trading experience? ▼

No. If you understand what a stock is and can use a computer, you can learn here. Osmosis is self-directed, so you learn live at your own pace — bring your questions to the calls and absorb as you go. (If you want a structured, step-by-step curriculum, that's the Full 1:1 Mentorship — see "Which Tier" above.)

How much time does it take? ▼

As much or as little as fits your life — that's the point. There's no curriculum to grind through. Show up to the live calls when you can (they're recorded if you can't), stay in the room, and ask questions as they come up. Osmosis is built for a lighter season.

How much does it cost? ▼

Osmosis is $300/month. You can step up to the full 1:1 Mentorship anytime you're ready for the curriculum and a personal roadmap, and step back to Osmosis whenever your season changes — no penalty either way.

What if I want the full curriculum later? ▼

Easy — just say the word and we'll move you into the full 1:1 Mentorship, where you get the complete curriculum plus a personal 12-week roadmap built with me. Osmosis is the on-ramp; you can go deeper whenever it's time.
